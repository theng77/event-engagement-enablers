About Us

Event Engagement Enablers is a Manila-based specialist in delegate acquisition, executive outreach, and audience engagement. We work closely with event organizers to ensure that every conference, summit, or forum is attended by high-value professionals and decision-makers.

Our mission is simple:

“Help event organizers deliver successful events by connecting them with the right delegates.”

We specialize in:

  • Delegate Acquisition – Recruiting senior professionals who match your target audience.
  • CXO & Executive Outreach – Connecting directly with CEOs, CIOs, CTOs, and senior decision-makers.
  • Audience Engagement – Building relationships with delegates before the event to boost participation.
  • Event Marketing Support – Amplifying your promotional efforts to reach the right people.
